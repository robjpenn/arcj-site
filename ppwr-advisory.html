The problem, stated plainly

This isn't a "read it later" regulation.

If you ship physical products into the EU — direct to consumers, to retailers, or between EU countries — your packaging is now in scope of PPWR. That means:

  • Obligations per country, not per company.Registration and waste-fee duties can arise in every EU country you sell into directly — not just where your business is based.
  • Design rules phasing in to 2030.Recyclability, labelling and minimum and maximum sizing requirements arrive on a staggered timeline, which means decisions you make on packaging now have a shelf life.
  • No obvious internal owner.It sits between legal, ops and whoever does the shipping — which is precisely why most SMEs don't know they have a gap.

We're not trying to alarm you. Most of this is manageable with the right sequencing. But it needs someone to actually look at it against your business, not a generic checklist.

FAQs
Does PPWR apply if I'm based in the UK?

Yes, if you place packaged goods on the market in an EU country. The obligation follows the parcel, not your registered address — UK sellers shipping direct to EU consumers are typically in scope from the first shipment.

Do I register in every country I sell into?

Extended producer responsibility registration and fees are administered country by country, so selling directly into several Member States can create obligations in each. Selling via a distributor or marketplace can shift who carries them — one of the first things worth establishing.

Is this a compliance service?

No — it's an advisory diagnostic. We tell you what applies, in what sequence, and where you need an accredited compliance partner, a solicitor or an authorised representative. We don't file registrations for you or give legal advice.
