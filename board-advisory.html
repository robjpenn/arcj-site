03 — Board Advisory

A senior sounding board, on your side of the table.

Practical counsel for owners scaling up — direct, honest input on the decisions that don't have an easy answer, from someone who has made them before.

Talk to us What's included
The situation

The business has outgrown what one founder can hold alone — hiring decisions, pricing calls, a partnership that could go either way. There's no board, no formal committee, just a growing list of decisions that deserve a second, senior opinion.

What we do

Ongoing counsel, on call — a regular working session plus direct access in between, so the big decisions get a second, experienced pair of eyes before they're made, not after.

What's included

A standing arrangement, not a one-off.

  • Regular working sessions: a standing cadence to work through what's live, not an annual review.
  • Direct access between sessions: a call or message away when a decision can't wait for the next one.
  • An honest second opinion: practical counsel from someone who has run the numbers before, not a generic governance checklist.

The same senior partner, every session. No rotating advisor, no committee.

FAQs

Questions we hear often.

Is this a non-executive director appointment?

It can be, but it doesn't have to be. Most clients want the substance — an experienced, commercially grounded voice on the decisions that matter — without a formal appointment. We shape it around what the business actually needs.

What kinds of decisions does this cover?

The ones that keep owners up at night: growth investment, structure, pricing, senior hires, succession, and exit readiness. Practical counsel, grounded in the full picture of the business — not governance theatre.

What does it cost?

Board advisory is typically a sustained, ongoing arrangement, sized to the business. The first conversation is free and we'll tell you plainly what it would cost — and whether we're the right fit — before anything begins.
